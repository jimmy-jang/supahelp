# Supahelp

Supahelp helps people investigate support cases personally and with colleagues. These terms distinguish a person's identity, a company's access rules and the work they share.

## Language

**Account**:
A person's Supahelp identity, which can belong to several organizations. An account is separate from a company's billing relationship and from the person's AI-provider account.
_Avoid_: Customer account, billing account, workspace account

**Organization**:
A company grouping accounts and workspaces, with its own seat capacity and policies. Membership in one organization grants no rights in another.
_Avoid_: Team, workspace, account

**Workspace**:
A work area containing cases and Playbooks. A team workspace belongs to one organization; a personal workspace belongs to the person using that computer.
_Avoid_: Organization, department

**Personal workspace**:
The free work area on a person's computer that requires no Supahelp account. Signing in does not move its content into an organization.
_Avoid_: Free organization, trial workspace

**Organization membership**:
An account's relationship to a company. It is distinct from a seat assignment and permission to enter a particular workspace.

**Workspace membership**:
An account's access to a particular workspace with a defined role. Organization owners additionally have access to every workspace in their organization.

**Seat capacity**:
The number of accounts an organization can accommodate under its purchased allowance or a maintainer-issued pilot grant. All workspace roles require capacity, including viewers and owners.
_Avoid_: Workspace seats, member-only seats

**Seat assignment**:
An organization's allocation of one seat to one account, covering that account's authorized workspaces in the organization. The same account needs a separate assignment in another organization.
_Avoid_: Global seat, portable subscription, workspace membership

**Seat reservation**:
An organization's temporary allocation of available capacity to an unseated invitation recipient. Pending invitations for the same known account share it; acceptance turns it into an assignment and its last pending invitation's expiry or cancellation releases it.

**Pilot grant**:
A limited, expiring seat allowance issued by the maintainers to a selected organization before payment collection. It is separate from a paid subscription and from customer-created organization setup.
_Avoid_: Payment, permanent free plan, automatic trial

**Organization owner**:
The account responsible for the organization's seats, billing and administration, with access to all its workspaces. This role does not confer another person's credentials or personal chats.
_Avoid_: Case owner, workspace admin

**Workspace admin**:
A role that manages settings and permitted membership operations within a workspace. It does not confer organization ownership or billing authority.

**Viewer**:
A seated workspace role that can read shared documents, answer questions addressed to them and flag customer data. It cannot perform general case editing or approve external actions.
_Avoid_: Free viewer

**Case owner**:
The person responsible for a case and its customer-facing actions. Organization or workspace administration does not replace the case owner's approval requirement.
_Avoid_: Organization owner

**Shared documents**:
Case documents and Playbooks available within a team workspace. Personal chats, raw tool output and credentials are separate from these documents.

**Team**:
A descriptive word for colleagues working together, rather than an additional organization or billing unit.
_Avoid_: Using team to mean a separate seat pool
